# Kit requests (what `@onigam/lab-kit` 0.5.1 lacks, from the physics and panel work of Solar Heater Lab)

Numbered, one paragraph each. Nothing here was worked around by patching the kit; each item says what the lab did instead.

1. **`sim/thermal` to move into the kit, with this lab's four new files.** `src/sim/thermal/` is the sand lab's core with two small changes (a `Math.abs` in `tubeFlow`, four exports) and four new files: `fluids.ts`, `solar.ts`, `loop.ts`, `layers.ts`, generic, tested on their own, with no lab's name in them (a test holds it). `docs/physics.md` §2 lists them. Another copy is being extended for the mass heater at the same time: whoever merges first takes the additions as they are. The stove can use `buoyantHead` for its stack.
2. **A closed-curve chart with a signed area.** The lesson's star and the panel's second chart: temperature against height round the loop, closed, the enclosed area signed (S17, Fig. 11). `LineChart` needs a growing x (it reads its range from the first and last points), and `CurveChart` is hard-wired for a power curve with an optimum. Until then the panel cuts the loop into two lines on one height axis: what goes up and what comes down, the area between them standing for the push, which reads well but cannot show a loop that folds on itself, as at a low tank. Asked: `ClosedCurveChart(points, { fillSigned: true, xUnit, yUnit })`. The stove's stack can use the same.
3. **Speed labels for a lab that runs on a calendar.** The strip writes `×3600`, `×86400`, which says nothing to a visitor, and more than five speeds overflow at 768 px. Sand Lab's request 4; still open. The lab passes five numbers; `holiday-week` and `boost-always-on` run at ×14400, which is not on the strip, so no speed lights up during them.
4. **A `readout` with a tag, and a chart unit.** Sand Lab's request 6. The panel appends `†` or `*` to the unit in `<small>`, and each chart's unit lives in its title because `yFmt` is cut above three characters at the left edge.
5. **`section` that starts closed, `num`, `months`, the fake DOM.** Mill Lab's requests 2, 3 and 5. `src/panels/test-dom.ts` is a copy of a copy, `num` a fifth version.
6. **A false-colour legend, and a shared ramp.** `tank-and-tap` colours ten layers live and `collector` a gradient along the risers, in °C, « false colour » said once. Sand Lab's request 8, answer in its request 9: the kit's `heatLegend` would save the third copy of `src/three/heat.ts`. For the 3D role.
7. **A long clock.** The third lab that writes the same `day of year → 21 Jun` in `src/panels/strip.ts`. Mill Lab's request 8.
8. **Dots on a true speed.** A 3D `FlowDots` that takes a signed speed in m/s and `view.slowMo`, from the state's `pipeSpeed`, with the sign reversing the dots. `lab-modeler` will copy Mill Lab's unless the kit has it first; the film contract asks that every motion take `view.slowMo`. This lab's dots run at 12 cm/s by day and under 1 cm/s at night, a range of more than ten to one, so a floor must say where it starts.
9. **A seeded `rng` outside three.js.** `rng` lives in `@onigam/lab-kit/three`, which the physics must not import, while the film contract names it for the engine. `src/sim/climate.ts` has its own mulberry32. Asked: export it from `@onigam/lab-kit/sim`.
10. **`PartRegistry.bounds` counts what is not drawn.** `Box3.setFromObject` takes hidden objects and `Points` too. A hidden stand's legs widened the `rig`'s overview, the zeroed buffer of a shower's drops pulled the `tank-and-tap`'s home to the origin. The lab folds the stand's hidden group to a point at the staff's foot, and keeps the water's points out of their part. Asked: the bounds of the visible meshes. The 3D role's.
11. **A world does not know which part is selected.** `update` has the state and the view, not the selection. So `test-stand`, hidden at y = 0 as research §0.4 asks, cannot appear when selected: the lab keeps its staff in view at all times, so that the outline has something to draw. Asked: `ViewOpts.selected`. The 3D role's.
12. **A lesson's `formula` in both languages.** `Lesson.formula` is a plain string: a formula with words in it (« y = bas du ballon − haut du capteur ») cannot be written twice, so lesson 4 falls back on symbols. Asked: `formula?: string | L`. The data role's.
13. **Part categories a lab names.** `PartCategory` has five values; a solar lab has collectors, stores and fixings that only fit by stretching `hydraulic` and `mechanical`. Mass heater's request 2, still open. The data role's.
14. **The kit's sun on a light card.** `SERIES_COLORS.pv` follows `--sun`, `#c99700` in light: 2.5:1 on `--card` (`#f8f9f6`), under the 3:1 a chart's line needs; the other three hold, from 3.7:1. In dark it is 7.6:1. This lab's one use, the air's dashed line, is now a grey of its own, `#78838d`, 3.7:1 on the light card and 3.9:1 on the dark one. Any lab that draws a line in `pv` has the same gap. Asked: a darker `--sun` for lines in light, or a `--sun-ink`. The lead's.
15. **An opaque picture.** `OutputPass` passes on the scene target's alpha, and three always asks the browser for a canvas with alpha, whatever `WebGLRenderer` is given. Alpha to coverage leaves a partial alpha at each card's edge, so the page's background is added to those pixels: pale dots round every olive, cypress and far tree on the light theme, hardly anything on the dark one. The same world looked different in each theme. The lab sets the canvas's own background to black in `src/main.ts`, which adds nothing. Asked: force the alpha to 1 in the last pass. Only this lab uses alpha to coverage today; any world that takes it up meets the same thing. The lead's.
