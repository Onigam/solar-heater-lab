# The setting of the `house` world

A generic Mediterranean village, no real house (research §3). Everything is procedural, DataTextures and geometry; no asset, so it builds in Node and the tests can see it. All of it seeded: the same village at every load.

| What               | How it is made                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | What it answers                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The house**      | Stone walls with real openings and reveals, so a window has depth; UVs in metres on every face, so a stone is the size of a stone. Rubble in courses of many widths, each stone its tone, a bump. Shutters in boards, sills, lintels, a door, a step, a zinc gutter and downpipe | « Buildings at scale »: eaves 5 m, ridge near 7.5 m (research §3, generic), tested                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **The slice**      | The house stops 1.72 m west of the machine's centre. Pale cut faces on the walls; the first floor's boards in section; the attic's boards; rafters and battens seen end on; rooms lined in plaster, a bathroom in tile                                                           | Research §0.4: the rafters under the cut roof, the way down to the bathroom                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **The roof**       | Real canal tiles: an instanced pan and an instanced cap, 720 of each over both slopes, each with its small error of place and its tone. Battens under each row, rafters at 600 mm, wall plates, two purlins, a ridge beam, mortared ridge tiles                                  | Close up it is tile, from afar it is rows, no bare plane                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **The olive**      | A thick, short, twisted bole, flaring into the ground; five limbs that fork twice; 45 clouds of about 64 leaf sprays, each card about 25 cm of narrow silver-green leaves, over a dark smooth mass in each cloud                                                                 | The jellyfish and the boulders of past reviews: no lumps seen as lumps, a trunk that branches. Its height 5 m stays under the collectors' foot, so **its shadow misses them in every site, all year: tested** over four sites, 25 days, every half hour |
| **Cypresses**      | A dark core, a flame of 620 small sprays                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Mediterranean at a glance; none stands in the slice's line of sight                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Far trees**      | Instanced crossed cards, a round cloud of dabs in the alpha, fuzzy edges, never within 60 m                                                                                                                                                                                      | Soft dabs on the hills, no faceted lump, no pill                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **The neighbours** | Stone or rendered houses, gables in their wall's stuff, a tile roof by texture and bump, framed and shuttered windows, all merged                                                                                                                                                | The village, at 12 m and more. No close-up was intended: they are the far detail                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **The ground**     | A height function, flat around the house, terraced hills beyond; vertex colours and the kit's `grassDetail`; terrace walls as long low bands                                                                                                                                     | Relief with banks; the camera's far plane is 1,800 m                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **The street**     | Packed cobbles, a jittered grid of many sizes, thin dark joints, near tones (the share of joints is tested)                                                                                                                                                                      | Not gravel as polka dots                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **The garden**     | A low stone wall with its gate, packed earth, three pots, a bench under the olive                                                                                                                                                                                                | Near detail, a place someone lives in                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

Windows light up at night: a warm emissive following the sky's night, decoration.

**Not done:** interiors beyond the bathroom, the kitchen's sink and the meter box. Vehicles, people, washing on a line: nothing asked them.
